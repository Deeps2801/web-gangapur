Stay Close to Devotion, Sleep in Comfort

Temple nearby accommodations such as dharamshalas, guest houses, and hotels usually provide both single bed and double bed rooms for devotees.

A single bed room is ideal for one person, offering basic facilities like a single bed, fan or AC, attached bathroom, and sometimes Wi-Fi, making it suitable for solo travelers seeking privacy and peace. On the other hand, a double bed room is designed for two people, typically couples or small families, and includes a larger bed (queen or king size), more space, and additional amenities such as hot water, TV, and wardrobe.

Some places also offer twin rooms, which have two separate beds in one room, allowing two guests to share comfortably. Overall, these rooms are affordable, clean, and located near temples, making them convenient for pilgrims to stay and attend prayers easily.
